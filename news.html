Australian Startup Lets Cooks Launch Their Own Branded Kitchens

A new Australian food startup is giving cooks a different way to start a food business — without first opening a restaurant.

SSU Australia is building a platform where cooks can create their own branded kitchen, build a menu and sell directly to local customers through pickup and delivery.

The idea is simple: many talented cooks already have the skill, the recipes and the kitchen. What they often do not have is the money or infrastructure needed to turn that into a business.

SSU Australia wants to help bridge that gap.

A cook might launch a small weekend kitchen, create a completely new food concept, test an idea they have always wanted to try, or begin building a brand that could eventually grow into something much bigger.

The company is now looking for its first group of founding kitchens in the Northern Rivers.

Experienced chefs, restaurant owners and talented local cooks are being invited to create a new kitchen concept of their own and become part of the first SSU Australia launch.

You don’t need a restaurant to start a food business. You need somewhere to begin.

To learn more or discuss becoming a founding SSU Australia kitchen, contact SSU Australia.
