How it works

A meal drop is simple.

One set menu. A limited number of portions. A clear pickup time. Local delivery if the host offers it.

1

Host cooks a set menu

A family recipe, comfort dish, regional meal, or weekly special.

2

Customers pre-order

The drop closes before cooking starts, so the host knows how much to make.

3

Pickup or local delivery

Customers collect nearby or choose delivery where available.

4

Reviews build trust

Customers review the food, accuracy, packaging and handoff.

For hosts

You bring the recipes. SupperTable helps with the setup.

This is for parents, retirees, students, new migrants, weekend cooks and talented families who already make beautiful food and want a proper way to sell it locally.

1

Apply as a host

Tell us where you are, what you cook, and whether you want pickup, delivery, or both.

2

Follow the setup steps

SupperTable guides you through the food safety and council registration steps that may apply.

3

Upload documents

Add certificates, registration details, kitchen checklist, menu information and allergens.

4

Create a meal drop

Set the menu, price, portions, pickup window and delivery area.

5

Sell after review

Your listing only goes live after SupperTable reviews the required steps.

For customers

Home-cooked meals without entering anyone's home.

Browse local weekly drops, check the menu and ingredients, pre-order, then pick up or get local delivery.

The food restaurants don't cook. Made by the families who do.

Restaurants are built for commercial menus. SupperTable is for family recipes, regional dishes, comfort food and small batches you may never find on a restaurant menu.

Safety

No one sells instantly.

SupperTable is not an open app where anyone can cook and start selling. Hosts must complete the required food safety and council steps before meals can go live.

Identity check Food Safety Supervisor certificate Council registration pathway Home business steps if needed Menu and allergen information Kitchen checklist Review before selling Customer reviews Incident reporting

No host can sell meals until the required steps are reviewed.

Hosts are independent food operators responsible for their own food preparation. SupperTable provides the website, checklist, order tools and document review.
