What is SupperTable?

Real home-cooked cultural meals, ordered before the drop closes.

Hosts cook a set menu.

Local cooks choose selected days, portions, prices, pickup windows and delivery options.

Customers pre-order.

Each drop closes before cooking starts, so hosts know exactly how much to prepare.

Meals are collected or delivered.

Food is picked up locally or delivered nearby. Customers do not enter anyone's home.

Hosts are reviewed first.

Required documents and checks are reviewed before any meals can go live.

Why not just order from a restaurant?

Restaurants are commercial kitchens. SupperTable is for the food families actually cook at home.

Restaurants often serve the same popular dishes. Home cooks can offer family recipes, regional dishes, comfort food, cultural meals, and limited-batch food you may never find on a restaurant menu.

For hosts

Turn your home kitchen into income.

SupperTable helps home cooks get set up properly, list limited-batch cultural meals, and sell them through local pickup or delivery.

You bring the recipes. We help with the setup, listing, orders and food safety pathway.
1

Apply as a host

2

Follow the setup pathway

3

5

Start selling after review

Your listing only goes live after SupperTable reviews your checklist and documents.

Safety and trust

No one sells instantly.

SupperTable is not an open app where anyone can cook and start selling. Hosts must complete the required pathway before their meals can go live.

Identity check Food Safety Supervisor certificate Council caterer registration Possible home-based business registration Menu and allergen information Kitchen checklist Admin review Customer reviews Incident reporting

No host can sell meals until the required steps are reviewed.

Hosts are independent food operators responsible for their own food preparation. SupperTable provides the guided platform, document workflow and marketplace.
